# Project Context

## Project Overview

This project is a complete redesign of the Lyftek website.

Current Website:
https://lyftek.in/

The existing website serves as a source of business information, service offerings, and company background. It should not be treated as the final design reference. Instead, analyze it to understand the business, identify strengths and weaknesses, and extract information that may be useful during the redesign process.

Whenever additional business information is required, review the current website before making assumptions.

---

# Project Objective

Redesign the Lyftek website from the ground up.

The goal is not to modernize individual UI components but to rethink the entire user experience, information architecture, visual presentation, messaging, and conversion strategy while preserving the company's core business identity.

Every design decision should support business objectives and improve user confidence.

---

# About Lyftek

Lyftek is a B2B technology consulting and software development company.

Rather than selling a consumer product or SaaS platform, Lyftek provides technology services and consulting to organizations looking to build, modernize, automate, secure, or scale their businesses.

The company positions itself as a long-term technology partner capable of delivering enterprise-grade software and digital transformation solutions.

---

# Primary Service Offerings

Lyftek provides services including, but not limited to:

- Custom Software Development
- AI & Machine Learning Solutions
- Generative AI Solutions
- Robotic Process Automation (RPA)
- Cloud Services
- Cybersecurity
- Managed IT Services
- IT Consulting
- Quality Assurance & Software Testing
- IT Staffing & Resource Augmentation
- Corporate IT Training

These services should be presented as interconnected capabilities rather than isolated offerings.

# Business Model

Business Type:

Business-to-Business (B2B)

Revenue Model:

Service-based consulting and software delivery.

Generate qualified business inquiries that convert into consultations, proposals, and long-term client engagements.

The website exists primarily as a business development and trust-building platform.

# Visitor Intent

Visitors usually arrive with a business challenge rather than casual curiosity.

Common motivations include:

- We need custom software.
- We need AI solutions.
- We want to automate business processes.
- We require cloud migration.
- We need cybersecurity expertise.
- We need experienced development teams.
- We need long-term technology partners.

They are evaluating whether Lyftek is capable of solving high-value business problems.

---

# Questions Every Visitor Wants Answered

Every important page should help answer one or more of these questions.

- What does Lyftek actually do?
- Can they solve my business problem?
- Have they handled similar projects?
- Can I trust this company?
- Why should I choose Lyftek over competitors?
- What industries do they work with?
- How experienced is their team?
- What is the next step to start working with them?

If a page does not help answer these questions, reconsider its purpose.

---

# Website Purpose

The website should:

- Build credibility.
- Establish trust.
- Demonstrate expertise.
- Clearly communicate services.
- Showcase technical capabilities.
- Present Lyftek as a reliable technology partner.
- Generate qualified leads.
- Encourage consultation requests.

The website should never feel like an online brochure.

It should function as an active business development tool.

---

# Desired Brand Position

The redesigned website should position Lyftek as:

- A Technology Solutions Company
- A Digital Transformation Partner
- A Trusted Enterprise Technology Consultant
- An Engineering-Driven Organization
- A Long-Term Strategic Technology Partner

The experience should communicate confidence, professionalism, expertise, and reliability.

---

# User Experience Goals

Every visitor should quickly understand:

- What Lyftek does.
- Who Lyftek serves.
- Why businesses trust Lyftek.
- How Lyftek solves business problems.
- What action they should take next.

Within the first few seconds of visiting the homepage, users should clearly understand the company's value proposition.

---

# Business Goals

The redesigned website should increase:

- Trust
- Professional credibility
- Qualified lead generation
- Consultation requests
- Contact form submissions
- Service inquiries
- Enterprise engagement

Every page should support one or more of these goals.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful redesign should achieve the following:

- Professional enterprise appearance
- Clear information hierarchy
- Strong visual credibility
- Better storytelling
- Improved service presentation
- Clear calls to action
- Excellent responsiveness
- Accessible user experience
- High implementation quality
- Easy future scalability

---

# Working Guidelines

Before proposing any design, feature, content structure, or user experience improvement:

1. Understand the business objective.
2. Understand the visitor's intent.
3. Validate assumptions against available information.
4. Review the current website if additional context is needed.
5. Recommend improvements based on UX principles rather than visual trends.

Every recommendation should strengthen trust, improve usability, and support Lyftek's business objectives.
