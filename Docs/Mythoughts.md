# Lyftek Website Redesign Strategy

# Business Understanding

## 1. What does Lyftek actually sell?

Lyftek is a **B2B IT Services & Software Solutions** company. They don't sell a product like an app or SaaS subscription.

They offer technology services to businesses.

### Offerings

1. Custom Software Development
2. AI & GenAI Solutions
3. RPA (Robotic Process Automation)
4. Cloud Services
5. Cybersecurity
6. Managed IT Services
7. IT Consulting
8. QA & Testing
9. IT Staffing / Resource Augmentation
10. Corporate Training

---

## 2. Who is their target audience?

Not students, not normal consumers.

### Primary

1. Business owners
2. Startup founders
3. SMEs
4. Enterprise companies
5. CEOs
6. CTOs
7. IT Managers

### Secondary

1. HR teams (looking for IT staffing)
2. Companies wanting employee training
3. Companies needing automation
4. Companies wanting digital transformation

These visitors are usually between **30–55 years old** and are evaluating vendors for serious business needs.

---

## 3. Why would someone visit this website?

A visitor typically has a business problem, such as:

1. "I need custom software."
2. "I need an AI solution."
3. "My business needs cloud migration."
4. "I need cybersecurity."
5. "I need developers."
6. "Can this company handle my project?"

They're looking for answers like:

1. Can I trust this company?
2. Have they done similar work?
3. What services do they offer?
4. Are they experienced?
5. How do I contact them?

The website's job is to build confidence and make contacting Lyftek easy.

### Lyftek Positioning

**Technology Solutions Company** or **Digital Transformation Partner**

**Purpose**

Lyftek is a B2B technology consulting and software development company whose website should build trust with business decision-makers and encourage them to contact the company for projects or consultations.

# Page Analysis

# 1. Home Page

## A) Navbar

### Current Implementation

1. Two-layer header (top contact bar + navigation)
2. Different background colors create visual separation

### Issues

1. Adds unnecessary height and visual complexity
2. Reduces emphasis on the main content
3. Doesn't match the cleaner aesthetic common among modern B2B technology brands
4. Company logo is not clearly visible on the navbar

### Recommendations

1. Replace it with a single unified navigation bar.
2. Keep contact details in the footer or Contact page.
3. Use the freed-up space for a stronger hero section and a clear primary CTA.

---

## B) Hero Section

### Current Heading

> Building Secure & Scalable Digital Solutions

### Issues

- Technically correct but very generic.
- Doesn't create a memorable identity.

### Recommended Heading

- We engineer businesses for the AI era.
- Engineering businesses for the AI era.

---

### Current Subheading

> We engineer custom software for Financial, RPA, AI, Enterprise, and Cloud-Based industries...

### Issues

- Too long
- Hard to scan
- Filled with technologies rather than business value

Visitors ask:

> "How will you help my business?"

The current copy answers:

> "Here are our technologies."

### Recommendation

> Custom software. AI. Automation. Cloud. Built for modern businesses.

---

### Certification Badge

ISO 9001:2015 & ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Certified

#### Positive

A strong trust signal.

#### Issue

Competes with the main hero message.

#### Recommendation

Move it into a dedicated **Trusted By / Certifications** section.

---

### CTA

Current:

- Explore our services

Recommendation:

Business owners usually want to:

- Talk to someone
- Get a proposal

Use stronger CTAs such as:

- Book a Consultation
- Start Your Project

---

### Hero Video

#### Advantages

1. Creates an immediate premium feel.
2. Adds motion and energy.
3. Reinforces a technology-oriented brand.
4. Makes the hero more engaging.

#### Disadvantages

1. Slower page load (especially on mobile)
2. Can distract from the headline
3. Often loops endlessly without adding information
4. Generic stock videos reduce uniqueness

#### Recommendation

Videos make more sense when the product itself is visual (automotive, real estate, fashion, travel, etc.)

Instead, use combinations of:

- Clean gradients
- Animated mesh backgrounds
- Moving grid patterns
- Subtle particle effects

## C) About Preview Section

Purpose:

- Introduce the company
- Explain why they exist

Current strengths:

- Good hierarchy
- Heading
- Tagline
- Description
- Image balancing the layout

### Improvements

1. Company name is unnecessary; visitors already know it.
   Use a heading like **Who We Are**.
2. Replace description with a modern AI-first narrative.
3. Too many services are listed here.
4. Replace the random photo with:
   - Technology ecosystem graphic
   - AI-inspired illustration
   - Trust-building visuals

---

## D) Services Section

Current observations:

- Each service has its own card.
- Images are good only when relevant.
- Descriptions are concise.

### Improvements

1. Improve hierarchy.
   Clearly communicate Lyftek's core strengths (AI, Software, Cloud, etc.)
2. Avoid random stock photos.
3. Use minimal cards.
4. CEOs and founders care about solving business problems rather than decorative visuals.
5. Rename section to:
   - Our Expertise
   - How We Help Businesses

**Inspiration**

# 3. Services Page

The Services page should introduce the entire service portfolio similarly to the Home Page.

## A) Individual Service Pages

Current information architecture is excellent.

### Improvements

Current paragraphs exceed 250 words.

Instead use:

- Heading
- 2–3 summary lines
- Highlights
- Key points

2. Reduce card usage throughout the page.

---

# 4. Solution Page

A dedicated **Solutions** page should be added.

**Inspiration**

## A) Individual Solution Pages

Strengths

- Outcome-focused headlines
- Excellent use of statistics
- Logical storytelling
  - Problem
  - Solution
  - Workflow
  - Results
- Stronger storytelling than generic service pages
- Industry-specific content

### Issues

1. Workflow shown as another card grid.
2. Repetitive descriptions reduce perceived depth.
3. Missing visual proof:
   - Dashboards
   - Product screenshots
   - Implementation examples
4. Needs stronger credibility through measurable outcomes and client success stories.

### Recommendations

1. Replace workflow cards with a visual timeline/process.
2. Add real product visuals:
   - Dashboards
   - AI workflow diagrams
   - Screenshots
3. Include measurable business outcomes.
4. Add case studies.
5. Use more varied layouts to avoid repetitive card designs.

---

### Improvements

1. Remove the hero banner.
