# Content Writing & Messaging Guidelines

> file existed yet at this path when referenced. Treat as authoritative for
> all website copywriting going forward, alongside
> `Design/design-engineering-os/11_CONTENT_STRATEGY.md`.

Do not generate generic IT company copy or rely on common marketing buzzwords.

The goal is to create messaging that reflects Lyftek's positioning as an enterprise technology and digital transformation partner, not simply a software development company.

## Content Principles

Every headline, paragraph, CTA, and section should:

- Speak to business outcomes rather than technologies.
- Address enterprise decision-makers (CEOs, CTOs, CIOs, Operations Heads, Founders).
- Communicate trust, expertise, clarity, and confidence.
- Be concise, modern, and easy to scan.
- Avoid clichés such as "cutting-edge", "world-class", "best-in-class", "one-stop solution", "innovative solutions", etc., unless there is a strong reason.

Always answer:

- What business problem is being solved?
- Why should a client trust Lyftek?
- What value does this section provide?
- What action should the visitor take next?

## Writing Style

Aim for the tone used by modern enterprise technology companies.

Use these as inspiration for messaging style and storytelling (do NOT copy their content):

- Stripe → Outcome-focused, simple, confident communication.
- Thoughtworks → Consulting-first messaging, business transformation, clear explanations.

Optionally study Microsoft Azure and Anthropic for clarity and professionalism.

## Expected Deliverable

For every section:

1. Explain the purpose of the section.
2. Define the message it should communicate.
3. Write multiple headline options where appropriate.
4. Write concise supporting copy.
5. Suggest an appropriate CTA if needed.
6. Ensure the copy flows naturally into the next section.

Focus on communicating business value, not listing technologies.

The final content should feel premium, enterprise-focused, and timeless, suitable for executives evaluating a long-term technology partner.
